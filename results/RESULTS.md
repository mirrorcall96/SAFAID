## 8. Consistency checks

- `average_19` stored by `eval.py` equals the mean of the 19 row accuracies for all 10 runs.
- The recomputed 19-row averages of the published baselines match the averages printed in the paper.
- For 10 of 10 runs, all 19 row accuracies (and real/fake accuracies and unknown counts) were recomputed from the 82,353 per-image predictions and equal the stored metrics.
- Unparseable answers (scored as errors): 7 of 823,530 answers across all 10 runs (r12: 7).
